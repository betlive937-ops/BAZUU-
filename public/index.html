BAZUU

Create trackable links and understand engagement through transparent visitor notices.

Visitor privacy and consent

Visitors can choose whether to allow basic click analytics when opening a BAZUU tracking link. If they decline analytics, they can still continue to the destination.

When basic analytics is accepted, BAZUU records the click time, browser and device category, and referring page when available.

BAZUU currently does not perform IP-based location lookups, store visitor IP addresses in its click records, request GPS coordinates, or access precise device location.
