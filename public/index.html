Approximate location information

When a visitor chooses to continue through a BAZUU tracking link, the service may use their network IP address temporarily to estimate their country, region and city. This estimate may be inaccurate and does not identify an exact neighbourhood or street address.

BAZUU does not request GPS coordinates or precise device location.
